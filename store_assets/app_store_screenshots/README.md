These sizes target Apple's current 6.9-inch iPhone portrait, 6.5/6.7-inch iPhone compatibility, and 13-inch iPad landscape screenshot slots. Raw UI captures are kept under `store_assets/raw_screenshots/`.

## Notes

- The screenshots use generated demo-library data, generated abstract album artwork, and synthetic daily listening records.
- Do not replace the demo artwork with third-party album covers unless the rights for App Store promotional use are cleared.
- Regenerate with `python tool/generate_app_store_screenshots.py` after refreshing raw screenshots.
