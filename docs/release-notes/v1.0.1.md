## Japanese App Store Release Notes

週間・月間の聴取レポート、前期間との比較、ホーム画面ウィジェットを追加しました。レポートは曲名を伏せて画像として共有できます。最終記録日時とiCloud同期状態も確認しやすくなりました。あわせて、聴取記録とiCloud同期の信頼性、App Lock、エラーからの復旧、アクセシビリティを改善しています。

## English App Store Release Notes

This update adds weekly and monthly listening reports, previous-period comparisons, privacy-aware recap image sharing, and home-screen widgets. It also makes record and iCloud sync health easier to check, while improving App Lock, error recovery, listening-record reliability, and accessibility.

## App Review Notes

- Temporary demo data remains separate from listening records and is never uploaded to iCloud.
- When Music access is denied, the app can open iOS Settings so the reviewer can grant access.
- App Lock remains locked if authentication times out or cannot be completed.
- Private iCloud history is removed locally only after CloudKit confirms the deletion.
- Premium purchase UI remains disabled in this release.
- The widget receives only a compact local recap through an App Group and does
  not access the Music library or CloudKit directly.
